Implement a versioned job contract with objective, inputs, dependencies, required capabilities, allowed adapters, acceptance criteria, budgets, deadlines, retry policy, priority, tenancy, privacy class, artifact destinations, human decision points, and cancellation semantics. Bind every field to project, task, revision, submitter, idempotency key, and durable record digests. Add a fail-closed checker, canonical fixtures, schema evolution rules, and hostile tests for stale, crossed, replayed, ambiguous, over-budget, private, and under-specified jobs. The contract must distinguish proposal, admitted, queued, running, blocked, completed, rejected, cancelled, expired, and terminal states.
