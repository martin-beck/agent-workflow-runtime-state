Define typed, versioned, capability-negotiated bridge protocols between the runtime and Coordinator/AWC, AWQ, AWG, and UI. Contracts must specify request/response schemas, event envelopes, revisions/digests, idempotency, timeouts, cancellation, retry boundaries, unknown/malformed outcomes, privacy-safe projections, compatibility policy, and ownership of every mutation. Coordinator remains authoritative for identity/claims/leases/terminal state; AWQ for requirement and quality outcomes; AWG for guidance/decision authority; UI for validated human decisions; runtime may execute, supervise, enforce, and project evidence only. Supply machine-readable schemas plus local positive/negative conformance fixtures, including stale, replayed, crossed-revision, missing, ambiguous, contradictory, and unauthorized mutation cases. No live bridge endpoint is required.
