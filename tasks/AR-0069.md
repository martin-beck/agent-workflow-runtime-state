Implement the executable runtime kernel that composes the durable job, scheduler,
evidence, accounting, and contractor contracts. Define the local storage and
transaction interfaces, revision/lease binding, event idempotency, recovery
bootstrap, and authority boundary. The kernel must be restartable and
testable with an in-memory or temporary local backend, but must not claim
Coordinator durability until the production transport is integrated.

Acceptance requires a runnable offline kernel API, explicit state transitions,
atomic event-plus-accounting writes, crash-safe recovery tests, schema/version
checks, privacy rejection, and no provider/network/LLM execution.
