# AR-0008: Privacy-safe event journal and provenance

## Objective

Define the redacted journal, provenance chain, retention, digesting, and public-safe evidence projection.

This is a design and conceptual boundary. Express the decision as a versioned specification under `specifications/`, validate it autonomously with positive and negative checks, and obtain review before implementation or oracle selection.

## Authority and integration

The Coordinator owns task identity, dependencies, claims, revisions, leases, and durable state. AWQ owns requirements, quality gates, adapters, and evidence acceptance. AWG owns uncertainty classification, oracle escalation, decisions, and reusable guidance. The runtime owns execution, supervision, isolation, normalized events, and bridges; it must not silently assume authority owned by those projects.

## Required work

- Define the normative contract, state machine, schemas, and failure semantics.
- Add a formal checker with positive and hostile cases, including stale revision, malformed input, replay, interruption, and unauthorized transition cases where applicable.
- Record exact task revision, specification digest, tool versions, and safe evidence for every result.
- Keep projections free of credentials, private paths, prompts, transcripts, and personal data.
- Update dependent ARs when the decision changes interfaces or constraints.

## Acceptance

The specification is versioned and machine-checkable; the checker passes positive cases and rejects hostile cases; evidence is reproducible from a clean checkout; authority boundaries are explicit; limitations and deferred decisions are recorded; and the AR remains consumable after completion through its status, dependencies, plan, evidence, and follow-up references.

## Next action

Implement the plan only after the specification review gate is satisfied. If confidence is insufficient or alternatives have materially different downstream effects, create or join an AWG discussion batch and do not guess.
