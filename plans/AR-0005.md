# AR-0005 plan

## Scope

Deliver the normative specification and the smallest tested implementation boundary for **Supervisor admission, lease, and worker lifecycle**. The result must integrate with the Coordinator Git backend, AWQ evidence workflow, AWG oracle protocol, and the runtime's provider-neutral contracts.

## Deliverables

1. A versioned specification with stable identifiers, invariants, inputs, outputs, errors, and compatibility rules.
2. An autonomous checker with positive, negative, replay, stale-revision, and recovery cases appropriate to the contract.
3. Implementation fixtures and privacy-safe evidence tied to the exact AR revision and specification digest.
4. Documentation of authority, dependencies, operational limits, security assumptions, and follow-up ARs.

## Dependencies

AR-0003, AR-0004

## Acceptance and review

AWQ must qualify the change and its evidence. Coordinator state must identify the exact task revision, claim, branch, checkpoint, and merge result. AWG must be used for unresolved design choices and must persist the final decision or reusable guidance. A clean checkout must reproduce the checker offline without credentials or hidden services.

## Non-goals

Do not add provider-specific policy to the runtime core, duplicate Coordinator/AWQ/AWG/UI authority, or treat an unverified remote observation as success.

## Handoff

After implementation, attach evidence, reconcile durable state, obtain independent review, and leave explicit follow-up ARs for any unresolved or newly discovered constraint.
