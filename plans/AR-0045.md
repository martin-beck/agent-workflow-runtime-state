# AR-0045 plan

## Scope

Qualify repeatable deployment, compatibility, observability, upgrade, rollback, incident recovery, and ongoing maintenance for production use. This plan is the next live integration layer after the completed offline contract series.

## Deliverables

1. Versioned normative specification with stable identifiers, bindings, lifecycle/state machine, compatibility, privacy, and failure semantics.
2. Standard-library offline model/checker with positive, hostile, stale, replay, interruption, and recovery coverage.
3. Minimal live implementation or integration harness behind explicit capability, credential, network, and human-approval gates.
4. Privacy-safe fixtures and evidence bound to AR-0045 revision 1, exact project/worktree identity, and tool versions.
5. Documentation of operator procedure, observability, rollback, limitations, and follow-up ARs.

## Dependencies

AR-0040, AR-0041, AR-0042, AR-0043, AR-0044

## Live-operation rules

Live execution may occur only through the approved Coordinator claim and lease, the exact project revision, and the isolated worktree. Credentials must be references or secret-manager handles, never evidence payloads. Network and provider access must be explicitly declared, bounded, cancellable, and auditable. If live infrastructure is absent, the AR remains locally qualified but live verification must be recorded as unverified.

## Acceptance and review

AWQ must independently qualify requirements and evidence. AWG must decide unresolved design alternatives and reusable guidance. UI decisions require a private revision-bound session and validated final event. Coordinator state must record claim, checkpoint, merge, release, reconciliation, and doctor evidence. Hosted CI, remote consumption, and provider success must not be inferred from local tests.

## Failure and recovery

Reject stale or crossed bindings, duplicate evidence, unauthorized transitions, privacy-bearing values, missing approvals, unbounded commands, and partial terminal claims. Interruptions must preserve a safe checkpoint or leave the operation explicitly blocked; recovery must fence the old worker and require a new valid lease.

## Non-goals

This AR does not redefine authority schemas, silently enable production access, or replace a separate security, quality, release, or human-decision gate.

## Handoff

After implementation, attach offline and live evidence separately, obtain independent review, merge at exact head, release the AR through handoffctl, reconcile generated state, and run doctor --live.
