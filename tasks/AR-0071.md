Implement the executable scheduler service over the AR-0062 model and AR-0069/
AR-0070 persistence. It must admit durable jobs, release dependencies,
dispatch fairly, reserve resources, issue and fence worker leases, heartbeat,
expire, retry, cancel, checkpoint, and reconcile terminal outcomes.

Every mutation must be revision-bound, journaled, accounted, idempotent, and
recoverable. Add deterministic concurrency, fairness, backpressure, starvation,
crash, and lease-fencing tests. Keep all provider execution disabled in this
AR.
