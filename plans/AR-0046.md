# AR-0046 production Coordinator transport

Deliver a versioned transport contract, standard-library offline server/client harness, request signing/auth-reference boundary, revision-fenced reads and writes, bounded retry policy, idempotent operation IDs, timeout/cancellation, and privacy-safe evidence. Reject stale revisions, crossed identities, replay with changed payload, malformed certificates, credential payloads, and ambiguous remote outcomes. Live transport remains gated and separately reported.
