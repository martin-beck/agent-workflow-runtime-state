# AR-0061 durable job contract

Define the machine-readable job envelope and state machine before scheduler implementation. Require exact Coordinator revision, project/worktree/session bindings, idempotency, dependency references, capability constraints, acceptance criteria, resource/time/cost budgets, retry and cancellation policy, evidence retention, privacy classification, and explicit human gates. Provide positive and hostile offline fixtures; no job is considered executable merely because its schema validates.
