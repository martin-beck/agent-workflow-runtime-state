Define a privacy-safe, revision-bound evidence projection for completed and interrupted autonomous development cycles. Capture autonomy level, agent roles, concurrency, interventions, blocked decisions, retries, failures, quality outcomes, time/resource budgets, recovery, and terminal disposition without raw prompts, transcripts, secrets, or private paths. Compare cycles across arbitrary consuming projects, identify recurring system-level friction, and produce proposal-only improvement findings. The runtime must never silently change its own policy or treat a case study as approval; Coordinator, AWQ, AWG, UI, and project authorities remain authoritative.
